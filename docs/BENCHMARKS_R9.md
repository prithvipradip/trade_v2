# R9 Benchmarks — first measured latency/accuracy/performance numbers (2026-07-11)

## Latency

**First-ever latency benchmark (effectively one clean trading day, 2026-07-09 — 07-10 was the known outage): the bot decides→places exit orders in ~170ms, but everything downstream is poll-bound — fills are detected ~33s after placement, the 30s risk monitor is blind 48% of market hours because the ~3-min scan sweep blocks the event loop, the full-universe scan runs every ~10.4min against a 5-min design, and per-symbol scans spend ~19 of ~20s wall time in completely untimed I/O; true exchange-fill latency has never been recorded (executions table: 0 rows).**

- **Exit decision -> exit order placed (fast_monitor_exit -> exit_order_placed)**: p50=0.170s, p95=0.290s, max=0.311s, min=0.165s (n=4)  
  _window: 2026-07-09 only (SPY iron-condor trailing stop 11:25 ET; AMZN/SPY/QQQ strangle macro-event flatten 14:22 ET); n=4 chains, the only exits in the surviving log window_ — Good — sub-second decision-to-wire is fine for a 30s-cadence retail bot
- **Exit order placed -> fill DETECTED (exit_order_placed -> exit_order_filled)**: p50=33.2s, min=33.0s, max=34.0s (n=4) — all were MKT orders (combo_exit_market_fallback), so this is detection latency, not fill latency  
  _window: 2026-07-09, n=4_ — Bad — market orders fill in <1s at IBKR; a competent shop is event-driven (orderStatusEvent/fillEvent) and confirms in <1s. 33s of not knowing your position after a stop-loss trigger is real risk
- **Fill detected -> fill processed (P&L booked, state updated)**: p50=0.106s, max=0.135s (n=4)  
  _window: 2026-07-09, n=4_ — Good
- **Full-universe scan sweep duration (8 symbols; SPY+QQQ run in a separate mini-cycle)**: 8-symbol sweep: p50=174.2s, p95=190.4s, max=193.6s (n=34). SPY/QQQ mini-cycle: 15-22s (n=34)

## Performance

**No formal performance benchmark exists; forensic extraction shows +$383.40 on 5 real closes (5/5 wins but 3 were forced macro flattens), 20.2% theta capture, beat cash 5.6x but captured only ~56% of SPY buy-hold over the same window, measured fill costs imply a 30-82% cost ratio vs the <33% viability bar, and the 50-close gate is statistically indecisive — it needs 42/50 wins just to confirm PF>1.0 at 90% confidence.**

- **Realized P&L, real closes**: +$383.40 net over 5 closes; per-trade mean $76.68, median $79.40, min $5.80, max $126.40, stdev $45.92; win rate 5/5 — but 3/5 exits were forced macro_event_flatten, 1 trailing stop, only 1 genuine take-profit  
  _window: entries 2026-06-30..07-07, exits 2026-07-06..07-09, n=5_ — Positive but not a target-hitting record — the exit engine was overridden by event risk on 60% of closes; sample far too small for any edge claim
- **Theta-capture efficiency (realized / max credit)**: 20.2% aggregate ($383.40 of $1,895 max credit); per trade 55.4% (the one real TP), 4.3% (trailing stop), 13.9-20.4% (the 3 flattens)  
  _window: same 5 closes, n=5_ — Bad vs the 40-50%-of-credit capture a competent premium-selling shop targets on winners; driven by event-driven early exits, not decay capture
- **Holding period**: mean 3.42d, median 3.01d, range 2.08-6.03d  
  _window: n=5 closes_ — Consistent with the 7-30 DTE short-premium design; fine
- **Commissions (hidden in P&L)**: $15.60 total = $2.60 round-trip per 2-leg strangle, $5.20 per 4-leg IC = 3.9% of the $399 gross on these closes; trades.commission column reads 0.0 on every row  
  _window: n=5 closes_ — Rate is normal IBKR; the accounting is bad — commissions are silently netted into realized_pnl while the commission field lies
- **Entry slippage**: -$89 across 8 fills = -$11.12/position average, worst 14.8% of credit on narrow ICs
