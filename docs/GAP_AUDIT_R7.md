### Missing for the money goal
- **[NOW / S]** TRUE IV rank/percentile from the already-stored daily ATM IV series, replacing the realized-vol min-max range rank in orchestrator._estimate_iv_rank (orchestrator.py:2268-2293). Compute percentile-of-history (not min-max) over stored implied_vol (historical.py load_daily_iv), fall back to the RV proxy only when <~120 stored IV days.
  - P&L link: Every credit strategy gates on iv_rank (iron_condor.py:75 floor 15, selector strategy fit); the variance risk premium — IV minus subsequently-realized vol — is THE documented source of short-premium PF, and it is harvestable only if entries concentrate when IMPLIED vol is rich relative to its own history. An RV range-rank instead concentrates entries right after realized vol spikes, i.e., after IV has often already normalized — selling ordinary premium while wearing elevated gap risk. At a PF>1.3 gate, systematically selling at median-or-worse IV percentile plausibly costs the 0.1-0.3 PF that decides the verdict.
- **[NOW / S]** Credit-to-width floor for ICs (e.g., total_credit >= 0.25-0.30 x max wing width) alongside or replacing the absolute $0.70 floor (iron_condor.py:144-146).
  - P&L link: With the flat 1.25x-credit loss limit, per-trade PF is a direct function of credit/width; the absolute floor admits wide-wing condors collecting 10% of width (terrible odds at 0.20 delta) and the wing-snap defects above make realized credit/width drift unmonitored. A ratio floor makes every accepted trade's risk-reward homogeneous — which is also what makes the 50-close sample interpretable.
- **[NOW / S]** Chain-snapshot quality telemetry: per get_chain call, log/persist n contracts, % with live two-sided bid/ask, % with IBKR modelGreeks vs BS-fallback, % with contract IV>0 — and a WARN when fallback share exceeds ~30%.
  - P&L link: Defects 1-2 are invisible today: nothing records that strike selection ran on median-IV fallback greeks or that legs had no live quote. One counter turns 'the verdict might be built on degraded chains' into an observable, protecting track-record trustworthiness — the actual product of this phase.
- **[SOON / S-M]** Ex-dividend calendar guard for short call legs (SPY/QQQ/IWM/XLE quarterly, TLT MONTHLY): block credit entries whose short call could be ITM at an ex-date inside the holding window, and flag open short calls with extrinsic < dividend the day before ex-date.
  - P&L link: Early assignment on a short ITM call the night before ex-div converts a defined-risk IC into short stock + long call in a $2.1k account — margin violation, forced liquidation, and an 'unmanaged position incident' that go-live gate 1 defines as disqualifying. TLT's monthly dividend gives ~12 windows/year on a universe name; earnings/macro calendars exist but no div calendar anywhere in data/.
- **[SOON / M]** OI via genericTickList '101' on a brief non-snapshot subscribe (or a slower once-daily OI pull per chain), un-deadening the min_open_interest filter live.
  - P&L link: OI is the one liquidity field that doesn't depend on today's tape; with volume gate at 0 and spread gate bypassable (defect 1), the bot currently has ~no real liquidity screen. OI>=10-100 cheaply excludes the strikes where exits get trapped at the 1.25x loss limit because there is no closing market — loss-limit slippage on trapped exits is pure PF leakage.
- **[SOON / S]** Adaptive snapshot wait in _fetch_ibkr_expiry: poll tickers until >=X% have two-sided quotes or greeks, up to ~3-5s cap, instead of a fixed 0.5s sleep; and backfill contract.implied_vol (not just greeks) in _calculate_greeks.
